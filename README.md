# Executive Sales & Profitability Overview

**Business Intelligence Project | Datheon Internship | Microsoft Power BI**

An interactive sales analytics dashboard developed as part of the Datheon internship, designed to evaluate sales performance, monitor profitability, and uncover insights across products, customer segments, and geographical markets.

---

## 1. Business Context

Understanding sales performance requires more than monitoring total revenue. Businesses also need to understand how sales evolve over time, which product categories generate the strongest results, which customers contribute most to profitability, and how performance differs across geographical markets and customer segments.

This project addresses these analytical needs through an interactive Power BI dashboard that consolidates key sales and profitability indicators into a unified reporting environment.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Monitor overall sales, profit, and profitability.
- Analyze quarterly sales and profit trends.
- Evaluate year-over-year sales and profit growth.
- Identify the most profitable product categories.
- Analyze product distribution across categories and subcategories.
- Identify top customers based on profit contribution.
- Compare profitability across customer segments.
- Evaluate sales and profit performance across geographical markets.

## 4. Data Modelling

The project uses a relational data model designed to organize sales transactions and supporting business dimensions for efficient analytical reporting.

### Model Architecture

4.1 Model Architecture

The model is centered around the Fact Orders table, which stores order-level information and connects to supporting tables describing customers, products, dates, and geographical responsibilities.

4.2 Relationships & Data Organization

Connected Fact Orders to the customer, product, and date dimensions to support multidimensional analysis.

Used Dim Date to enable quarterly and yearly sales and profitability analysis.

Incorporated the People table to support regional and personnel-related analysis.

Included the Returns table to make return-related information available for further analysis.

## 5. DAX Development

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) was used to develop the dashboard's core business metrics, financial KPIs, and time-based calculations.

### 5.1 Sales & Order Metrics

Measures were created to evaluate overall sales performance and order activity.

**Total Sales**
```dax
Total Sales =
SUM('Fact Orders'[Sales])
```

Calculates total sales revenue across the selected filter context.

**Total Orders**
```dax
Total Orders =
DISTINCTCOUNT('Fact Orders'[Order ID])
```

Counts the number of unique orders.

**Total Customers**
```dax
Total Customers =
DISTINCTCOUNT('Fact Orders'[Customer ID])
```

Counts the number of unique customers.

### 5.2 Profitability Analysis

**Total Profit**
```dax
Total Profit =
SUM('Fact Orders'[Profit])
```

Calculates total profit generated by the selected orders.

**Profit Margin**
```dax
Profit Margin =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Profit],
    [Total Sales],
    0
)
```

Measures profit as a percentage of total sales.

### 5.3 Time Intelligence & Growth Analysis

Time intelligence calculations were implemented using the dedicated `Dim Date` table to compare current sales performance with the previous year.

**Sales Previous Year**
```dax
Sales Previous Year =
CALCULATE(
    [Total Sales],
    SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR('Dim Date'[Date])
)
```

Calculates sales for the equivalent period in the previous year.

**YoY Sales Growth**
```dax
YoY Sales Growth =
DIVIDE(
    [Total Sales] - [Sales Previous Year],
    [Sales Previous Year],
    0
)
```

Calculates the percentage change in sales compared with the previous year.

### 5.4 Key DAX Functions Applied

| DAX Function | Application |
|---|---|
| `SUM()` | Aggregating sales and profit |
| `DISTINCTCOUNT()` | Counting unique orders and customers |
| `DIVIDE()` | Calculating ratios and growth percentages safely |
| `CALCULATE()` | Evaluating measures under modified filter context |
| `SAMEPERIODLASTYEAR()` | Comparing performance with the equivalent previous-year period |

These measures provide the foundation for the dashboard's KPI cards, trend analysis, customer metrics, and profitability comparisons.

## 7. Key Business Observations

The following observations are based on the results visible in the dashboard.

### Overall Performance

The dashboard reports:
- Total Sales of 2.30M.
- Total Profit of 286.40K.
- Profit Margin of 12.47%.
- YoY Sales Growth of 46.88%.

These indicators provide a snapshot of the overall business performance.

### Sales Growth

The year-over-year trend shows a strong improvement in sales and profit growth in 2017 compared with 2016, followed by a more moderate growth rate in 2018.

This highlights the importance of monitoring both revenue growth and profit growth rather than relying on sales figures alone.

### Product Category Performance

Office Supplies leads in both order volume and profitability, accounting for approximately 50.79% of total profit. It represents the strongest contributor to overall category-level profit.

Furniture ranks second in order volume but contributes a comparatively smaller share of total profit.

Technology, despite recording a lower order volume than Office Supplies and Furniture, generates approximately 42.77% of total profit.

These findings suggest that order volume alone does not determine a category's contribution to profitability. Technology generates a substantial share of total profit despite having fewer orders, while Furniture's relatively high order volume translates into a smaller profit contribution.

### Customer & Segment Analysis

The customer and segment analysis evaluates individual customer contributions to profitability and compares profit margins across customer segments.
Tamara Chand is the highest-ranked customer, generating approximately $9.0K in profit.
The segment comparison reveals differences in profitability across the three customer segments.
Home Office records the highest profit margin at 14.03%.

### Geographical Performance

The geographical analysis compares business performance across five cities using total orders, total customers, and total profit.
New York City leads the selected cities, recording 450 orders, 355 customers, and approximately 62.04K in profit.

## 8. Business Recommendations

8.1 Optimize Product Category Profitability
Maintain the strong performance of Office Supplies by identifying and prioritizing its most profitable products and subcategories.

Investigate Furniture's relatively low profit contribution despite its high order volume, focusing on product costs, discounting practices, and pricing strategies.

Further analyze Technology's high profit contribution to identify products with strong profitability and opportunities to expand their sales.

Monitor both order volume and profit contribution when evaluating product category performance.

8.2 Strengthen Customer Profitability Management
Prioritize customer retention initiatives for high-profit customers.

Analyze purchasing patterns and product preferences among the top five customers to identify opportunities for cross-selling and upselling.

Monitor customer-level profitability over time to identify changes in contribution.

Avoid focusing exclusively on revenue or order volume when evaluating customer value; consider profit contribution as well.

8.3 Improve Profitability Across Customer Segments
Review Consumer segment pricing, discounting, and product mix to identify potential margin improvement opportunities.

Monitor segment margins regularly to identify unfavorable profitability trends.

8.4 Optimize Geographical Performance

Maintain and investigate the factors supporting New York City's strong performance.

Analyze Seattle's relatively high profit contribution compared with its order volume to identify potentially valuable product or customer patterns.

Investigate opportunities to improve profit contribution in Los Angeles, considering its substantial order volume.

Evaluate customer activity and product mix in San Francisco and Detroit to identify opportunities for growth.

Consider profit per order and average profit per customer as additional geographical performance indicators.

8.5 Monitor Sustainable Sales Growth

Monitor sales and profit growth together to ensure that revenue expansion translates into sustainable profitability.

Investigate the business factors behind the strong 2017 performance.

Analyze the slowdown in 2018 to identify potential changes in sales volume, product mix, or customer contribution.

Use quarterly performance monitoring to identify emerging trends and support timely business decisions.

Overall Recommendation

The analysis suggests that business performance should be evaluated through a combination of sales volume, profit contribution, and profit margins rather than relying on revenue or order volume alone.

Prioritizing high-performing categories, investigating profitability gaps, strengthening customer relationships, and monitoring geographical performance can help support more informed commercial decisions.

## 9. Analytical Approach

The project follows a Business Intelligence workflow:

1. **Business Understanding:** Identify the key sales and profitability questions.
2. **Data Preparation:** Prepare and organize the source data using Power Query.
3. **Data Modelling:** Structure the data and relationships to support analysis.
4. **DAX Development:** Create measures for financial KPIs and analytical comparisons.
5. **Data Visualization:** Build interactive visuals and KPI indicators.
6. **Business Analysis:** Interpret the results and identify performance patterns.
7. **Recommendations:** Translate findings into potential business actions.

## 10. Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can transform sales data into an interactive analytical solution.

By combining KPI monitoring, time-based analysis, product performance, customer segmentation, and geographical analysis, the dashboard provides a consolidated view of business performance.

It also demonstrates the importance of moving beyond descriptive reporting toward actionable business insights.
